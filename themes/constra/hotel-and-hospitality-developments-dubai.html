Hotel & Hospitality Development Project Management

Hotel construction in Dubai and the UAE is one of the most demanding forms of development a client can undertake. The projects are large, the stakeholder relationships are complex, and the expectations are high on every side. Operators impose brand standards. Authorities require regulatory compliance. Investors expect cost and programme discipline. And the contractor needs to be managed with clarity and rigour throughout.

Tanmu Project Management Services has direct experience delivering hospitality developments in the UAE, including projects managed on behalf of clients with Marriott International at Dubai Culture Village and Al Jaddaf. We understand what hotel construction actually involves at the operational level, not just in principle.

Our role is to provide independent project management on behalf of the developer or investor. We do not act for the contractor, the operator, or the design team. We act for you.

Independent Oversight for Hospitality Developments

Hotel projects create a particular dynamic in which multiple parties with competing priorities are working simultaneously on the same development. The operator wants the design built to brand standards. The contractor wants to manage programme and protect margin. The design team wants the concept delivered intact. Without independent representation, the developer absorbs the risk when these interests diverge.

Tanmu provides structured governance throughout the full project lifecycle. We establish clear reporting frameworks, monitor contractor performance against agreed milestones, manage the cost plan, and ensure that design intent and operator requirements are maintained through construction. Our responsibility is to the client. That is the only interest we represent.

Managing the Complexity of Hotel Construction

Hotel projects involve a level of co-ordination that goes well beyond most other building types. Guest room layouts, back-of-house operations, food and beverage facilities, public areas, MEP infrastructure, and FF&E procurement all need to be managed concurrently, within a single construction programme, and in alignment with operator approval processes.

International hotel brands typically impose strict design standards that govern everything from corridor widths to ceiling heights to fire compartmentation. These standards must be validated at design stage and enforced through construction. Any deviation identified late in a project is expensive. Catching it early requires an experienced team with the attention and authority to act.

Tanmu has managed this process directly on Marriott-branded developments in Dubai. We understand how operator liaison works in practice, including the approval cycles, the scope of FF&E and OS&E procurement, and the co-ordination required between the construction team and the incoming operator during the final stages of a project.

Hotel Development in Dubai and the UAE

Dubai remains one of the leading global destinations for hospitality investment. Demand for branded hotels, apart-hotels, and serviced residences continues to grow across the emirate and into Abu Dhabi, Ras Al Khaimah, and beyond. Successful delivery of these projects depends on experienced, independent project management to co-ordinate international design teams, local contractors, and demanding hotel operators within a structured governance framework.

Tanmu supports hospitality developments across the UAE, working with investors and developers who require clear project oversight and disciplined cost and programme control from concept through to handover.

Hotel Development Project Management Includes:

  • Development Planning & Feasibility: Early-stage project planning including development strategy, programme structuring, and co-ordination with hospitality operators and design consultants.
  • Design Co-ordination: Managing collaboration between architects, interior designers, MEP engineers, and hotel operators to ensure the design meets both operational and regulatory requirements throughout all stages.
  • Operator Liaison: Managing the relationship between the developer and the hotel brand, including design approval processes, FF&E and OS&E procurement co-ordination, and pre-opening requirements.
  • Procurement & Contractor Selection: Structured tendering and appointment of contractors and specialist subcontractors, with transparent evaluation against cost, capability, and programme.
  • Cost Control & Financial Monitoring: Continuous budget oversight, valuation review, variation assessment, and transparent financial reporting throughout the construction phase.
  • Programme Management: Development and monitoring of detailed construction programmes with early intervention where slippage is identified.
  • Authority Approvals: Co-ordination of approvals with Dubai Municipality, DDA, Civil Defence, and other relevant regulatory bodies involved in hospitality developments.
  • Quality & Technical Oversight: Regular site inspections to ensure compliance with approved drawings, brand specifications, and contract obligations across all trades.
  • Handover & Defects Management: Structured close-out procedures, snagging co-ordination, and documentation control to ensure a clean handover to the operator.

You Should Know

On a hotel development, an independent project manager acts on behalf of the developer or investor to co-ordinate the design team, contractor, and hotel operator. Their role is to maintain control of cost, programme, and quality while managing the competing priorities of each party. They also oversee authority approvals, procurement processes, and the handover of the completed building to the incoming operator.

Hotel construction involves multiple interdependent scopes running concurrently: structure, MEP, fit-out, FF&E, and landscaping all need to be co-ordinated within a single programme. On top of this, international hotel operators impose strict brand design standards that must be approved at each design stage and enforced through construction. Managing these requirements alongside contractor performance requires experienced, dedicated oversight.

Hotel developments in Dubai typically require approvals from Dubai Municipality, the Dubai Development Authority (where applicable), Dubai Civil Defence, and the Department of Economy and Tourism for hotel licensing. Each authority has its own submission requirements and approval timelines, which must be carefully managed alongside the construction programme to avoid delays at handover.

FF&E stands for Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment. OS&E stands for Operating Supplies and Equipment. These procurement scopes cover everything from guest room furniture and light fittings to kitchen equipment and linen. On branded hotel developments, both scopes are subject to operator approval and often represent a significant portion of the total project budget. Managing FF&E and OS&E procurement alongside construction requires careful scheduling to ensure items arrive and are installed at the right stage of the programme.
