Independent Construction Project Management in Dubai and the UAE

We deliver world class project management

Tanmu Project Management Services is a boutique project management consultancy based in Dubai providing independent client representation and construction project management services across the UAE. We work with private investors, developers, and property owners to oversee luxury villas, hotels, residential developments, and industrial facilities from early planning through to completion.

Our role is to protect our client’s interests throughout the construction process. This includes programme management, cost control, contractor oversight, authority coordination, and quality monitoring to ensure projects are delivered on time and within budget.

Vendor Selection

Sectors We Serve

Construction Expertise Across Key Markets

Our project management consultancy has delivered projects across a range of construction sectors in Dubai and the wider UAE. Whatever the project type, our approach remains the same: protect the client's interests, control cost and programme, and deliver to the agreed standard.

Residential & Villa Projects

From new-build luxury villas on Dubai Hills and Emirates Hills to full-scale renovation projects in Arabian Ranches or Palm Jumeirah, we provide complete project management for private residential developments. We oversee design co-ordination, contractor selection, cost control, and quality monitoring from concept through to handover.

Hotel & Hospitality Developments

We have a strong track record managing hotel developments in the UAE, including projects with Marriott International. Our team co-ordinates all phases of hotel construction, fit-out, and operator liaison, ensuring the finished product meets both brand standards and the client's commercial objectives.

Learn More

Residential Apartment Buildings

Multi-unit residential developments demand close co-ordination between developers, contractors, and authorities. We provide independent oversight across the full project lifecycle, managing programme, cost, and quality to ensure the development is delivered on time and within budget.

Learn More

Industrial & Manufacturing Facilities

Industrial construction carries its own set of technical demands, from specialist MEP requirements to regulatory compliance and authority approvals. Our team has managed the delivery of factory and manufacturing facilities in the UAE, bringing the same rigour in cost and programme control that we apply across all sectors.

Learn More

Commercial & Office Fit-Out

Office and commercial fit-out projects require careful co-ordination to minimise disruption and keep costs under control. We manage the full process on behalf of the building owner or tenant, from initial scope definition and vendor selection through to handover, ensuring the space is delivered to specification and on programme.
