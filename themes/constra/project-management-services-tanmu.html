Construction Project Management Services

Full lifecycle oversight for construction projects across the UAE

Tanmu Project Management Services provides specialist construction consultancy for high-value residential, commerical and industrial projects across Dubai, Ras Al Khaimah, and the wider UAE. Our services cover every stage of the project lifecycle, from initial feasibility and budget setting through design, procurement, construction, and final handover. We work exclusively on behalf of the client, not the contractor, ensuring that every decision made on your project is made with your financial interests and quality expectations at the centre. With over 30 years of experience delivering multimillion dirham projects, our team provides the expertise, independence, and rigour that luxury villa construction demands.

Conceptualisation Stage

The conceptualisation stage is where the financial and strategic foundations of your project are established. We define the project brief, set the cost framework, select the consultant team, and prepare the documentation required to move into design with clarity and confidence. Decisions made at this stage have the greatest influence on total project cost, making expert guidance here the highest-value investment of the entire process.

  • Feasibility Studies
  • Conceptual Design Review
  • Design Standards
  • Cost Estimating
  • Project Team Selection
  • Funding Documents

Creation Stage

The creation stage covers design development, technical direction, and procurement preparation. We review designs for cost alignment and buildability, co-ordinate MEP and specialist consultant inputs, manage the vendor selection process, and ensure the project is compliant with all relevant UAE authority requirements before tender. This stage determines the quality and accuracy of the documentation your contractors will price against.

  • Owner's Representation
  • Design Development Review
  • Technical Direction
  • Energy Optimisation
  • Vendor Selection
  • Compliance Review
  • Technical Integration

Execution Stage

The execution stage is where budgets are won or lost. We manage contractor procurement, certify payments against verified progress, control variations, monitor quality at every stage of construction, and co-ordinate the testing, commissioning, and handover process. Our active site oversight ensures the project is delivered to the agreed standard, within the agreed budget, and on the agreed programme.

Our Services in Detail

What each service covers and how it protects your project

Owner's Representation

Owner's Representation is the most important appointment you make on any UAE construction project. Our Owner's Representatives act exclusively on your behalf throughout the entire project lifecycle, managing consultants, challenging contractor claims, certifying payments, and making all critical decisions without the conflict of interest that arises when the same firm is also managing delivery. We maintain a strict separation between our Owner's Representation and project management functions, ensuring that your interests are never compromised by the project's momentum.

Project Management

Our project management service covers the full co-ordination of your construction project from contract award through to practical completion. We manage the programme, co-ordinate the consultant and contractor teams, track progress against the agreed schedule, and report regularly on cost, quality, and time performance. Our project managers bring direct UAE experience on luxury residential and commercial projects, with the technical knowledge to challenge decisions that could affect your budget or programme.

Cost Estimating and Quantity Surveying

Accurate cost planning is the foundation of every successful UAE villa project. Our quantity surveying team prepares detailed cost plans at each stage of design, prepares Bills of Quantities for tender, evaluates contractor submissions, and manages the cost throughout construction through rigorous variation control and monthly cost reporting. We challenge design decisions that push costs beyond the agreed budget and present value engineering alternatives that maintain quality whilst protecting your financial position.

Vendor Selection

Choosing the right contractor is one of the highest-stakes decisions on any construction project. Our vendor selection service covers the full procurement process: prequalification of contractors against defined criteria, preparation and issue of tender documents, management of the clarification process, evaluation of submissions, and support through commercial negotiations. We have no commercial relationships with any contractor or supplier we recommend, ensuring the selection process is entirely independent and focused on your best interests.

Quality Monitoring

Quality failures in villa construction are rarely visible until after the walls are closed and the finishes are applied. Our quality monitoring service establishes a structured inspection regime from the start of construction, covering first fix MEP, structural works, waterproofing, finishes, and all specialist installations. We verify that the standard of work certified for payment matches the standard specified in the contract, and we document all defects formally so that they are remedied before handover rather than after.

Energy Optimisation

In the UAE climate, energy performance is not a sustainability aspiration. It is a direct operating cost. A poorly specified HVAC system in a Dubai villa can consume 30–40% more energy than a well-designed one, with that cost recurring every year of the building's life. Our energy optimisation service reviews mechanical and electrical specifications against the building's specific orientation, glazing, occupancy pattern, and usage profile, identifying improvements that reduce long-term running costs without compromising the specification or the design intent.

Luxury Villa Development

Building a luxury villa in Dubai requires the same level of professional oversight as a large-scale commercial development, but with a level of personal attention to detail that matches the ambition of the project. Our luxury villa service covers the full development process from concept to completion, including brief development, consultant team selection, design co-ordination, contractor procurement, construction management, and handover. We have delivered villa projects across Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills, Jumeirah Islands, and Abu Dhabi, with built-up areas ranging from 1,000 to over 3,000 square metres.

Other Services

In addition to our core project management disciplines, Tanmu provides energy and sustainability consultancy and detailed design reviews focused on improving the long-term operability and maintainability of completed developments. These reviews identify opportunities to reduce operation and maintenance costs, protect building assets, improve the occupant experience, and align the development with sustainability objectives. They are particularly valuable for clients planning to hold or let a property after completion, where long-term running costs are a direct investment consideration.
